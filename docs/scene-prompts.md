# Scene prompts (Grok Imagine)

Each scene is one short clip that the site plays frame by frame as you scroll.
Make the still first, then animate it ("Make video") from that still.

**Rules for every scene**
- Landscape 16:9, the highest resolution Grok offers.
- One slow, continuous camera move. No cuts, no zoom jumps, no shaking.
- No text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI or numbers anywhere in frame.
- No people and no faces.
- Near-black background (#060609) with light only from the subject: violet, cyan and amber.
- The clip should **end on a calm, composed frame**; that is where the scroll stops.

Save each clip as `<id>.mp4` in `C:\ml\PortFolio_Build_Gem\scenes-src\`, then tell Claude.
These are illustrations: the site labels them so, and none is presented as a lab photo.

## 1. `precitec`: two surfaces (home PRECITEC chapter + project page)
**Still:** Macro cinematic photograph of a cylindrical brushed-metal optical sensor head pointing straight down at a thin glass microscope slide on a matte black lab bench. A thin cone of white light leaves the lens and splits into a rainbow fan before reaching the glass; a cyan line of light rests on the top surface of the glass and an amber line on the bottom surface. Shallow depth of field, deep black background, faint dust motes in the beam, 85mm lens, studio product lighting, photoreal.
**Video:** Slow dolly in and down toward the glass slide while the rainbow fan of light gently brightens and the cyan and amber lines on the two glass surfaces sharpen. Dust drifts slowly. No cuts.

## 2. `triagemate`: order from a flood (home TriageMate chapter + project page)
**Still:** Thousands of small translucent glowing paper cards floating chaotically in a dark void, lit in amber and soft violet, cinematic volumetric light, shallow depth of field, photoreal, abstract.
**Video:** The floating cards slowly drift and then fall into three neat parallel lanes of light that stretch into the distance, as the camera glides forward along the lanes. Smooth, calm, no cuts.

## 3. `forecast`: the band ahead (home "More work" chapter, Veltrix and Asset pages)

**Video:** The camera glides low and steadily forward along the glowing ridge line toward the widening band of light on the horizon. No cuts.
